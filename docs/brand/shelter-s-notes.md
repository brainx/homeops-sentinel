# Negative-space Sentinel S — October 15, 2026

Client requested shaping the lower-left negative space like an S for Sentinel. This proposed iteration carves a curved S into the home perimeter using a transparent SVG mask. The S is subtracted from the mark, not drawn over it in a fixed background color. The roof and right wall preserve the home silhouette.

Rendered desktop study inspected. No horizontal overflow at 390, 768, and 1500px. Both SVG assets parse; same mark appears flat, reversed, and on the app-icon field. At 16px the S is a small detail rather than a fully legible letter; recognition should be evaluated at actual use sizes before final adoption. Previous studies are preserved.
