# Sentinel Signature — October 15, 2026

Proposed direction responding to the request for a more impressive execution of the home outline and negative-space S.

The broad geometric S replaces the curved letter cut into the lower-left corner. The S becomes the primary interior opening, with its lower stroke opening through the base. The roof, left upright, right upright, and right foundation retain the house silhouette. The home and S are meant to read together rather than as an icon with a typographic addition.

The master is a monochrome SVG with a transparent subtraction mask. The app-icon concept applies a restrained mint finish to the same geometry on an evergreen field. The page includes a reversible construction reveal, a comparison with the previous study, and actual-size studies at 16, 24, 40, and 64px. Display lockups do not rename the product.

Verification performed: rendered and inspected desktop presentation; no horizontal overflow at 390, 768, or 1500px; construction reveal opens and resets, including button state; no browser script errors; SVG XML parses; 1024px transparent PNG and app-icon PNG exports checked for dimensions; the center of the master PNG is transparent, confirming genuine negative space. The S is visually clearer at 24px and above; a dedicated favicon may still need optical adjustment. These are production craft checks, not user recognition testing or trademark clearance.
