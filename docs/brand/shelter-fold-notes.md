# Shelter Fold — home-outline revision

October 15, 2026. Proposed, not approved.

Client direction: a cooler and more recognizable logo containing an outline of a home.

The symbol gives the home a bold roofline, a generous central opening, and a diagonal cut into the lower-left perimeter. Flat and reversed versions preserve the geometry; the app-icon concept adds a restrained mint treatment over evergreen. This is an original vector study, not trademark-cleared artwork.

Rendered and inspected the desktop study; verified no horizontal overflow at 390, 768, and 1500px. The board includes 16, 24, 40, and 72px studies. These are visual checks, not recognition testing. The 16px silhouette may need optical adjustment for a production favicon. No production app files were changed.

Files: shelter-fold.html, shelter-fold-mark.svg, shelter-fold-app-icon.svg, shelter-fold-preview.png. The earlier studies remain available for comparison.
