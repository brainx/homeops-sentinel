# Open Design refinement record

The client explicitly requested a design-agent pass. A separate reviewing agent read the open-design skill, inspected the source and rendered desktop/mobile console, and recommended retaining the identity.

Applied improvements:

- Mobile brand occupies its own row; navigation follows beneath it.
- Mobile metrics reflow instead of compressing three columns; validation gets a full row.
- Finding selection becomes compact rows with reduced secondary metadata.
- Operational text is 14px; supporting metadata is generally 12px. Mobile textarea is 16px.
- Unknown evidence has neutral styling; each finding controls its relevant source reference.
- The presentation-only mobile icon stage is smaller, with its palette legend hidden at narrow widths.
- Tab controls use a single tab stop and arrow/Home/End navigation.

The logo geometry was preserved. This was a scoped craft and usability review, not a new identity exercise or a formal accessibility audit.

## Independent follow-up verification

The reviewing agent revisited the approved library and console at 390×844 and 1440×1000. It confirmed the mobile hierarchy, readable text, contextual source/reference states, neutral unknown statuses, initial tab stops, functioning patch preview, and truthful local-note feedback. No overflow, broken images, JavaScript/HTTP errors, or new material issues were found. The identity remained intact.
